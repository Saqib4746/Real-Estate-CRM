Design should be clean, spacious, and highly professional.

Use minimal gradients with emerald green, royal blue, and gold accents.

Avoid bright colors, cartoonish icons, and clutter.

Maintain consistency across all screens.

Use premium card layouts, modern tables, elegant typography, and smooth interactions.

The final prototype should feel like a production-ready enterprise real estate management platform suitable for developers, dealers, investors, and sales teams, with a special focus on **multi-society management**, **interactive color-coded plot maps**, and a **professional booking form supporting logos, image uploads, camera capture, and document management**.
